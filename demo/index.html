What is built in

You could build a blog from scratch. These templates start with the parts that take the longest to get right. Stack: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Markdown posts.

Structured data and canonical links

JSON-LD, canonical links and social preview images are generated from every post, to help search engines and AI assistants understand each page.

llms.txt, sitemap and RSS

A sitemap, an RSS feed and an llms.txt file are generated from your posts. Nothing here guarantees rankings or citations in AI answers.

Faster discovery with IndexNow

Add an IndexNow key and each template can ping IndexNow when you add a post, so search engines that support it can hear about the page.

Yours to keep

MIT licensed, on your own domain and your own Vercel account. Change anything you like.

Small details handled

Light and dark mode, readable on a phone, keyboard friendly, and optional GA4 analytics behind a consent banner: Google Analytics does not load until a visitor agrees.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a design and deploy it in one click on your own Vercel account. It asks for one setting, NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL.

  2. 2

    Add Markdown posts with front matter, or edit the sample posts.

  3. 3

    Point your domain at it. The sitemap, feed, llms.txt and structured data update as you add posts.

Optional: publish from your AI assistant

scatterpost is a separate tool, with a free plan and paid plans, that publishes to your blog first, then cross-posts with the canonical link pointing back to your site. Tested with Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini; publishing through ChatGPT is not yet tested. The templates work fully without it.
